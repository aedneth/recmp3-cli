# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Our Standards

**Positive behavior includes:**
- Being respectful and constructive in discussions
- Welcoming newcomers and first-time contributors
- Focusing feedback on ideas and code, not people
- Accepting criticism gracefully

**Unacceptable behavior includes:**
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Trolling or sustained disruptive behavior
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by emailing **eduardoa.borjas@gmail.com**. All reports will be handled with confidentiality.

Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code of conduct.
